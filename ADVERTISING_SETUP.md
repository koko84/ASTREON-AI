# ASTREON AI — Google advertising preparation

## Important
This project is not approved by Google and this file does not guarantee AdSense or AdMob acceptance.

## Website (AdSense)
Before applying:
- Publish a working website on a domain you control.
- Add substantial, original, useful public content (for example, original AI tutorials, explainers, and product guides).
- Provide clear navigation and working About, Contact, Privacy Policy, and Terms pages.
- Explain what data is processed by the AI service and how users can contact the operator.
- Do not publish copied, thin, automatically generated filler, or misleading content.
- Keep ads clearly distinguishable from controls and content. Never ask visitors to click ads.
- Do not place AdSense ads on pages where private communication is the primary focus, such as the private AI chat screen. Use ad placements only on eligible public content pages after review.

## Android (AdMob)
- AdMob is separate from AdSense.
- Publish the finished app in a supported store and link it in AdMob.
- Complete the app readiness review and the app-ads.txt verification steps where required.
- Add the official SDK and ad unit IDs only after the AdMob account and app setup are ready.

## Account age
Google says AdSense publishers must be at least 18. If the site owner is under 18, a parent or legal guardian can apply using their own Google Account; payments go to that adult if approved.

## Placeholder component
`src/AdPlaceholder.jsx` is a visual placeholder for future eligible public content pages. It is intentionally not mounted in the private chat interface. It is not a live ad and contains no publisher ID or ad unit ID.
